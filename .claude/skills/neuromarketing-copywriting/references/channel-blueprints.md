## Usage
Pick one blueprint and adapt to the user goal.
Do not mix multiple long-form frameworks in one draft.

## 1) Landing Page Hero + Core Sections

### Hero Block
- Headline: Specific promised outcome for one audience segment.
- Subheadline: Mechanism and timeframe with a believable boundary.
- Primary CTA: One action verb and one friction reducer.
- Trust strip: Logos, ratings, usage count, or short proof line.

### Problem-Agitation-Solution Section
- Problem: Name concrete current pain.
- Consequence: Clarify cost of inaction.
- Solution: Explain new mechanism in plain language.

### Objection Section
- List top objections with direct counter-proof.
- Use data, testimonial, process transparency, or guarantee details.

### Offer Section
- Explain what is included.
- Show value stack and anchor.
- State price and terms clearly.

### CTA Section
- Restate result.
- Clarify first step.
- Reduce perceived risk.

## 2) Sales Email (Single Offer)
- Subject: Curiosity plus relevance, no clickbait.
- Opening: Pattern interrupt tied to audience pain.
- Body A: Problem and cost of delay.
- Body B: Mechanism and proof.
- Body C: Offer and clear CTA.
- Close: Low-friction next step and timeline.

Constraints:
- Keep one email to one core intent.
- Remove extra links that split attention.

## 3) Paid Ad Copy (Meta/LinkedIn)

### Primary Text Structure
- Hook in first line.
- Pain or aspiration framing.
- Mechanism in one sentence.
- Proof micro-snippet.
- CTA.

### Headline
- 4-9 words with concrete value.

### Description
- Clarify who it is for or what happens next.

Constraints:
- Ensure first line survives truncation.
- Keep claims verifiable.

## 4) Search Ad (Google)
- Headline 1: Main keyword + value.
- Headline 2: Differentiator.
- Headline 3: CTA or trust cue.
- Description: Problem, mechanism, proof, CTA.

Constraints:
- Match keyword intent and landing page promise.
- Avoid vague superlatives.

## 5) Product Description (Ecommerce)
- Opening: Who it is for and immediate benefit.
- Mechanism: How it works in practical terms.
- Benefit bullets: Outcome-focused, concrete.
- Objection block: Fit, quality, shipping, return policy.
- CTA and reassurance.

Constraints:
- Lead with use-case language, not only specs.
- Balance emotion and utility.

## 6) 30-60s Video Script
- 0-5s: Hook and audience callout.
- 5-20s: Pain and cost of status quo.
- 20-40s: Mechanism plus proof.
- 40-55s: Offer and CTA.
- 55-60s: Risk reversal or confidence line.

Constraints:
- Keep spoken rhythm natural.
- Use one claim per sentence.

## 7) Retargeting Message
- Acknowledge prior touchpoint.
- Address one unresolved objection.
- Add new proof or angle.
- Use direct CTA.

Constraints:
- Do not repeat cold message.
- Reward prior attention with higher specificity.

## Microcopy Checklist (Any Channel)
- Button labels: action + value ("Start Free Audit", "See Demo Plan").
- Form labels: clarify what user gets after submit.
- Error messages: reduce anxiety and give immediate next step.
