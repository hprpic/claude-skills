# Quality Rubric (100 Points)

## How to Use
Score each criterion from 0 to 10, then apply weight.
Revise weakest criteria first.
Target score: 85 or above before delivery.

## Weighted Criteria
- Clarity and Fluency (15): Is copy easy to understand at first read?
- Audience Relevance (15): Does copy mirror real audience pains and goals?
- Value Proposition Strength (10): Is the promise specific and differentiated?
- Specificity and Concreteness (10): Are claims bounded with numbers, mechanisms, or constraints?
- Credibility and Proof (15): Are key claims supported by evidence?
- Objection Handling (10): Are major doubts addressed explicitly?
- Emotional Resonance (10): Does copy create felt urgency or aspiration without hype?
- CTA Precision (10): Is there one clear next action with low friction?
- Ethical and Compliance Safety (5): Is messaging honest, non-deceptive, and policy-safe?

## Scoring Guide
- 0-3: Weak or missing.
- 4-6: Present but generic or inconsistent.
- 7-8: Strong and persuasive.
- 9-10: Excellent, specific, and hard to improve.

## Revision Protocol
1. Fix any score below 6 before polishing style.
2. Replace generic adjectives with proof or mechanism details.
3. Remove extra CTAs and competing intents.
4. Tighten long sentences and abstract nouns.
5. Re-score after each revision pass.

## Common Causes of Low Scores
- Benefits are stated but not proven.
- Promise is broad and non-specific.
- Emotional intensity exceeds factual support.
- Copy uses urgency without real reason.
- CTA asks for too much too soon.

## Final Delivery Gate
Ship only when all are true:
- Total score >= 85.
- No criterion below 6.
- Compliance safety passes.
- At least 2 viable variants prepared for testing.
