# Ethics and Compliance Guardrails

## Purpose
Use persuasion to improve clarity and decision confidence, not to exploit vulnerability.

## Hard Red Lines
- Do not fabricate data, testimonials, usage numbers, or endorsements.
- Do not use fake scarcity, fake countdowns, or false deadlines.
- Do not promise guaranteed outcomes.
- Do not make medical, legal, or financial claims without compliant evidence and required disclaimers.
- Do not imply official approval when none exists.

## High-Risk Claim Patterns
Flag and replace phrases such as:
- "guaranteed results"
- "risk-free forever"
- "works for everyone"
- "instant transformation"
- "scientifically proven" (without citation)
- "limited spots" (if not truly limited)

## Safe Alternatives
- Replace absolutes with bounded, evidence-based phrasing.
- State context and constraints ("for teams with ...", "in most cases where ...").
- Use confidence language tied to mechanism ("designed to", "helps", "can reduce").
- Use explicit uncertainty when needed ("results vary by implementation quality").

## Scarcity and Urgency Rules
Use scarcity only when it is factual and auditable.
Allowed:
- Real inventory limits.
- Real enrollment deadlines.
- Real capacity limits.
Not allowed:
- Evergreen false countdowns.
- Repeated "last chance" campaigns with no real constraint.

## Proof Standards
Prefer proof in this order:
1. First-party performance data with timeframe and sample context.
2. Verifiable testimonials with role, use case, and outcome.
3. Independent certifications, audits, or expert validation.
4. Transparent mechanism explanation when hard proof is limited.

## Sensitive Domains
If copy touches regulated domains (health, finance, legal, employment, children):
- Ask for jurisdiction and policy constraints.
- Use conservative claims.
- Recommend legal/compliance review before publication.

## Ethical Persuasion Checklist
- Is the message true?
- Is it clear what is promised and what is not?
- Is user autonomy preserved?
- Is risk explained proportionally?
- Is CTA aligned with user benefit?

If any answer is no, revise before delivery.
