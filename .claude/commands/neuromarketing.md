## STEP 2: DEEP NEUROMARKETING ANALYSIS (silent — do not show to the user)

Before writing, conduct this analysis internally:

### A) AWARENESS LEVEL (Eugene Schwartz)
Unaware → Problem Aware → Solution Aware → Product Aware → Most Aware
The text strategy depends on this step.

### B) TRIUNE BRAIN — Which brain are we writing for?
- **Reptilian / Brainstem** — survival, safety, threat. Decides in milliseconds. Cannot be rationalized.
- **Limbic system** — emotions, memory, identity, desire. Processes BEFORE language. Drives 95% of actual purchase decisions.
- **Prefrontal cortex (mPFC)** — rational justification, price comparison. Engages only AFTER emotional buy-in.

**Rule:** Marketing that targets only the mPFC (features, specs, logic) fails because it's talking to the wrong audience. The brainstem and limbic system vote first.

### C) POLYVAGAL THEORY (Porges) — Nervous System State
Identify which state the text puts the reader in:

| State | Mode | Buyer Behavior |
|-------|------|----------------|
| **Ventral Vagal** | Safe, socially engaged | Open, curious, trusting, ready to buy |
| **Sympathetic** | Fight/Flight | Scanning for threats, price-resistant, skeptical |
| **Dorsal Vagal** | Freeze/Shutdown | Numb, scrolling without engagement, unable to decide |

**Key insight:** A website, ad, or email that activates subtle threats (aggressive headlines, confusing navigation, no social proof, hidden terms) pushes the visitor into a Sympathetic or Dorsal state — and a person in those states CANNOT buy. They freeze or flee.

**Safety signals (Ventral Vagal triggers):**
- Familiar faces, warm tones, clear structure
- Social proof that reduces uncertainty
- Clear guarantee, no hidden terms
- Understandable navigation, no overload
- Tone that demonstrates understanding, not manipulation

**Threats (Sympathetic/Dorsal triggers):**
- Aggressive headlines implying catastrophe
- Too many options at once (paradox of choice)
- Unclear CTA
- Feeling that something is being left unsaid
- Extreme urgency + high price = paralysis

### D) SOMATIC MARKERS (Damasio)
Antonio Damasio proved: **decisions are made through bodily sensations, not rational analysis.** The body votes first — gut feeling IS the decision-making mechanism.

Primordial feelings (pleasure/pain, tension/relaxation, warmth/cold) arise in the brainstem and precede every conscious choice.

**Applications:**
- Copy that creates a **physical sensation** encodes deeper than claims
- Text that the body feels converts better than text the mind thinks
- "I just like this brand" = Damasio's somatic marker system, not irrationality

**Somatic language examples:**
- "Feel the stability of the steering wheel" → activates somatic simulation
- Image of steam rising from coffee → triggers sensation of warmth
- "Take a deep breath..." → parasympathetic activation = safety

### E) MIRROR NEURONS — Contagion Mechanism
Specialized cortical neurons that fire identically whether you're watching or performing an action. The neurological basis for why:
- Testimonials with emotion transfer credibility
- UGC (real users) outperforms polished brand ads
- A model smiling while using a product makes viewers feel the same
- "Before/after" works: viewers simulate both states in their own nervous system

### F) WINDOW OF TOLERANCE (van der Kolk)
Optimal arousal zone for engagement and decision-making:
- **Too calm** → no motivation, boredom, no action
- **In the window** → alert, curious, emotionally engaged, can decide
- **Hyper-aroused** → panic, overwhelm, resistance, flight — brain cannot process information
- **Hypo-aroused** → shutdown, numbness, dissociation, scrolling without perceiving

**Application:** Fear appeal and urgency work **only within the window**. Too intense a threat = mPFC goes offline = freezing or abandonment. That's why extreme scarcity + extreme fear + extreme pressure = cart abandonment.

### G) AMYGDALA vs mPFC
- **Amygdala** = smoke detector. Threat in milliseconds. Cannot distinguish real danger from perceived danger (aggressive headline, confusing CTA).
- **mPFC** = rational override. Can calm the amygdala IF the person feels sufficiently safe.

Under stress, the mPFC SHUTS DOWN. The amygdala takes over. A person in amygdala-mode cannot read benefits, compare prices, or make considered decisions.

### H) MEMORY ENCODING (van der Kolk)
Arousal + emotion = deeper memory. Adrenaline released during emotional experiences chemically encodes memory more clearly — up to a threshold.
- **Mildly emotional ads** = good recall, stored as narrative memory
- **Highly emotional ads** = strong recall, stored as sensory fragments (image, sound, bodily sensation) — replayed in sleep, reinforced
- **Overwhelming ads** = memory breaks down, thalamus shuts off sensory integration, nothing encodes

**The most persuasive ads don't leave a rational argument — they leave a sensory imprint.**

### I) COGNITIVE BIASES FOR THIS SITUATION
Select the 5–7 most relevant:
Loss Aversion, Social Proof, Scarcity/Urgency, Authority, Reciprocity, Anchoring, Commitment & Consistency, Curiosity Gap, Framing, Decoy Effect, Peak-End Rule, Von Restorff Effect, Zeigarnik Effect, Fluency Effect, Hyperbolic Discounting, Pattern Interrupts, Unity (Cialdini 7th), IKEA Effect

### J) EMOTIONAL ARC (Plutchik)
- Headline: Surprise or Anticipation
- Problem: Fear or Sadness (with efficacy — not without a way out)
- Solution: Trust + Joy
- CTA: Anticipation + Excitement

**NOTE ON SHAME:** Shame causes FREEZE, not action. Any copy that implies "you're not good enough without this" risks a freeze response, not a purchase. Never use shame as a motivator.

## STEP 3: WRITING THE COPY

### STRUCTURE (select based on text type)

**For Sales Page / Landing Page:**
```
1. HEADLINE (above the fold) — Curiosity gap + Loss aversion + Self-referential
2. SUBHEADLINE — Clarifies and deepens + Ventral Vagal signal (it's safe to read on)
3. HOOK/STORY — Narrative transportation, Mirror neurons, PAS formula
4. PROBLEM AMPLIFICATION — Agitate pain within Window of Tolerance, specific details
5. VISION — BAB formula (After state), Dopamine anticipation, somatic simulation
6. BRIDGE — Why this solution (Dual process, System 2 logic as confirmation)
7. PROOF — Social proof (C-R-A-V-E-N-S), Authority, Mirror neurons (real people)
8. OFFER — Value stacking, Anchoring, Decoy effect in packages
9. GUARANTEE — Risk reversal, Polyvagal safety signal (reduces Sympathetic activation)
10. CTA — Benefit-framed, First-person, calibrated urgency (within Window of Tolerance)
11. PS — Peak-End rule, repeat the key benefit or sensory imprint
```

**For Email:**
```
Subject: Curiosity gap / Pattern interrupt / Personalization (4–7 words)
Preview: Deepen curiosity, don't repeat the subject
Opening: Hook in the first sentence (RAS filter), safety immediately
Body: Micro-story with PAS or BAB + somatic language
CTA: One clear call, benefit-framed
PS: Recency effect — repeat the most important thing or open a loop
```

**For Product Description:**
```
- Sensory language (activate sensory cortices — sight, touch, sound, smell, taste)
- Concrete benefits > Abstract features (Dual Coding Theory)
- Embodied cognition metaphors
- Social proof integrated (Mirror neurons)
- Specificity as trust signal (Ventral Vagal)
- "Imagine..." mental simulation
```

**For Social Media Post:**
```
- Hook in the first 1–2 sentences (F-pattern, RAS filter)
- Pattern interrupt opening
- High-arousal emotion within Window of Tolerance (Awe, Surprise, Humor)
- Open loop or Curiosity gap
- Stories > Facts (Narrative transportation, Mirror neurons)
- Clear CTA at the end
```

### MANDATORY LANGUAGE ELEMENTS

**1. Power Words (by category):**
- Curiosity: "secret", "we reveal", "what 97% don't know", "forbidden"
- Urgency: "now", "immediately", "today", "disappearing"
- Safety: "guaranteed", "verified", "safe", "risk-free"
- Exclusivity: "exclusive", "only for", "selected"
- Authority: "research shows", "according to a study"
- Value: "free", "bonus", "worth €X"
- Personalization: "you", "your", "for you"

**2. Sensory language** — Activate a minimum of 3 senses through concrete descriptions. Bodily sensation encodes deeper than claims.

**3. Concreteness** — Never "fast results" when you can say "within 17 days." Never "many clients" when you can say "4,312 clients." Specificity = Ventral Vagal signal = trust.

**4. Active voice** — Minimum 90%. Passive voice = psychological distance = Sympathetic activation.

**5. Rhythmic structure:**
- Short sentences for impact. Like this.
- Long sentences for building a narrative that guides the reader through the argument.
- Rule of Three: "Faster. Cheaper. More effective."
- Anaphora: "You know when... You know that feeling... You know what you need then..."

**6. Self-Referential Processing** — Text written for the reader, not about the brand. "You" > "We." The protagonist is the reader.

**7. Social Proof (C-R-A-V-E-N-S):**
Credible + Relevant + Attractive + Visual (name, position) + Enumerated (numbers) + Nearby (industry) + Specific (result in numbers)

**8. Pricing (if relevant):**
- Anchoring: Show higher value BEFORE the offer
- Value stacking: "You get X (worth €Y) + A (worth €B)..."
- Frame as "investment"
- Break down to daily amount
- Guarantee with concrete terms (specificity = trust)
- Decoy effect (3 options, mid-tier as decoy)

**9. Scarcity and Urgency — ONLY if authentic, CALIBRATE within Window of Tolerance:**
- Quantity scarcity: "Remaining: 3 spots" (specific)
- Time scarcity: "Until Friday at midnight"
- Dopaminergic anticipation: "This opens on Monday..."
- **NEVER** combine extreme fear + extreme urgency = paralysis and flight
